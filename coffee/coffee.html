My coffee shop favorites

Iced honey cinammon latte
with oat milk

My go to everyday coffee order.
You can never go wrong with ordering this anywhere.

Matcha Lemonade
My favorite drink during the warmer days, this is perfect for when you want something light, and not as milky like a latte.
Hot vanilla latte with oat milk
Pretty much any latte with oat will do it for me, but a hot vanilla latte on a cold day is such a good pick me up.
Cold brew with a splash of oat milk
Sometimes the extra strength and bitterness of a cold brew is the only thing that can get me through my day.
Oat milk cortado
I love a cortado because it is the perfect ratio of espresso to milk. I love the glasses they come in and the latte art is always so satisfying on these.
Blueberry muffin
The best coffee shop pastry ever. I love to snack on one of these when I am sticking around to study or meeting a friend.
