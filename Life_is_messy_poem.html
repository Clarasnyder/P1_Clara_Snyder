As much as we strive to have it all,
just so-our ducks in a row.
as much as we portray our image as neat
that somehow-we have it at our feet.
life is messy...
We say what we mean,
layered with pain that is seen,
coupled with trauma that has been
and drop it as trash to wean.
life can be messy...
we recall our intentions,
regret our deflections,
deny our detentions
and continue our inventions.
